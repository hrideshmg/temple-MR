# KoviLens

Mixed-reality app for Meta Quest 3 / 3S that places a model of Moovar Kovil (Kodumbalur, early Chola, 9th century CE) in your room. You can hold and scale it, demolish it into rubble that lands on your real furniture, rebuild it, and watch a narrated story tour of its architecture. Several headsets in one room can share a session (in progress).

Unity 6000.5.5f1 · URP · Meta XR SDK 207 · Netcode for GameObjects
